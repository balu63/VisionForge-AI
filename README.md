# VisionForge AI

**Imagine it. Prompt it. Create it.**

A premium AI-powered creative studio where users can generate images and videos from text prompts.

## Features

### ✅ Fully Working Features
- **Real AI Image Generation** - Generates actual AI images from your prompts
- **Video Generation** - Creates video content from text descriptions
- **Image to Video** - Upload an image and animate it with AI
- **Download** - Download generated images and videos
- **File Upload** - Drag & drop or click to upload images
- **Gallery** - View all your creations
- **History** - Complete history of all generations
- **Favorites** - Save your favorite creations
- **Share** - Share creations via URL or native share
- **Settings** - Customize generation preferences
- **Dark/Light Theme** - Toggle between themes
- **Responsive Design** - Works on all devices
